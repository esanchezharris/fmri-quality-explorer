Sources and display limitations

This browser-only portfolio publishes derived display means, atlas labels, mask membership, regional observations and selected saved analysis outputs. Downloaded 4-D BOLD sources remain in the local research workspace. The demo performs no new cleaning or connectivity analysis.

Study data

Richardson, H., Lisandrelli, G., Riobueno-Naylor, A. & Saxe, R. (2018). Development of the social brain from age three to twelve years. Nature Communications 9, 1027. The original OpenNeuro ds000228 version 1.0.0 is distinguished from the OSF derivatives. The original dataset description records PDDL. No new derivative version is inferred from the original version.

Tutorial data: Nilearn development-fMRI fetcher, OSF derivative collection and preprocessing notes. The tutorial uses 4-mm downsampling and stored-code quantization after fMRIPrep. Temporal variation is a proxy on that grid, not a brain mask or proof of biological signal.

Independent SPM8 archive: normalized 2-mm display grid with prior 5-mm smoothing. This is a separate preprocessing lineage, not the exact parent of the tutorial. It does not recover native 2-mm acquisition. The exact matching 2019 fMRIPrep parent BOLD/mask/HTML QA remains unavailable. Source filenames and SHA-256 identities are recorded; each viewer's evidence export records its selected source identity and hashes.

Atlas

Harvard–Oxford cortical atlas, cort-maxprob-thr25-2mm, 48 regions, no hemisphere splitting of the analysis atlas. Supplied MNI x coordinates define left/right display strata. Harvard Center for Morphometric Analysis; distributed with FSL. The official FSL license notice identifies Harvard–Oxford as CC BY-SA 4.0. Adaptations here are nearest-neighbor resampling to the display grids and colored parcel selection; these atlas-derived materials retain CC BY-SA 4.0. The lookup and source hash are preserved in exported provenance. This portfolio does not relicense the other third-party material.

Viewer

NiiVue 0.69.0, Copyright (c) 2021, Niivue, BSD 2-Clause. Full license and bundled library provenance. Rendering uses mean BOLD, not a structural scan or a functional activation map. Colored parcels are atlas selections. Display interpolation and rotation do not add acquired detail or change any counts.

Methods and added work

The workflow adapts Nilearn atlas extraction, fMRIPrep confound selection and sample-mask cleaning. Ordinary Pearson correlations are an explicit change from the example's default shrinkage estimator. The inventory, integrity rules, original-frame audit, separate coverage measurements, provenance, unavailable-result handling and reporting are project additions.
