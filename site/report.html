Saved findings from a descriptive movie-watching demonstration using eight public adult scans. This is an excerpt of verified outputs, not a new analysis or a validation study.

Question and methods

How does censoring FD strictly above 0.5 mm change retained data and regional Pearson correlations? Both conditions exclude frame zero and supplied non-steady-state frames. The atlas and nuisance model are fixed. Project guardrails require 120 retained seconds and 20 nominal residual design degrees of freedom. Full methods.

Coverage investigation

For sub-pixar123, the independent SPM8 mask included 171/647 atlas centers in region 14 and 50/610 in region 37. The region-37 split was 49/316 left and 1/294 right. In the tutorial derivative, 20/76 region-37 centers varied over the run despite a computable regional matrix. Sub-pixar124 had 0/82 varying tutorial region-14 centers and 0/647 centers in the independent archived mask.

The tutorial variation proxy and independent SPM8 mask measure different things with different denominators. The matching 2019 fMRIPrep parent mask and QA remain unavailable. These observations establish a coverage concern; they do not isolate acquisition, masking, registration, resampling or quantization contributions. No primary result or exclusion was changed.

Sub-pixar130's archived mask had 152 undefined locations, all outside the selected cortical atlas. Unknown membership remains unknown. Coverage review bins are project conventions, not validated exclusions. Changed matrices do not prove biological accuracy.
