Question

What changes when high-motion frames are excluded?

The same atlas and nuisance model are used twice. Both conditions exclude frame zero and supplied non-steady-state frames; the comparison adds only FD > 0.5 mm. The saved workflow found seven paired matrices and one unavailable result.

01 / sub-pixar123

A matrix can hide limited coverage.

Its correlations were computable, but the independent SPM8 mask included just 171/647 atlas centers in region 14 and 50/610 in region 37. Region 37 had 49/316 left versus 1/294 right.

02 / sub-pixar124

A flat signal stays unavailable.

Region 14 had 0/82 varying tutorial centers and 0/647 centers in the independent archive mask. Its correlations were withheld. Motion and retention remain visible.

03 / the evidence

Keep different denominators separate.

Temporal variation on the tutorial grid is a proxy. The independent archive mask measures spatial inclusion. They are separate preprocessing lineages; the matching fMRIPrep parent mask and QA remain unavailable.
